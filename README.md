<h3>🚗 AI-Based Acoustic Vehicle Fault Detection</h3>

<p>
My graduation thesis focused on detecting potential vehicle faults through acoustic signal analysis and artificial intelligence.
</p>

<p>
The system analyzes short vehicle recordings to identify anomalies related to the <strong>engine, bearing, and axle/CV components</strong>. The trained models were integrated with a FastAPI service and a Flutter mobile application prototype, transforming academic research into a functional end-user product.
</p>

<strong>Key contributions</strong>

<ul>
  <li>Audio preprocessing and acoustic feature extraction</li>
  <li>Machine learning and deep learning model development</li>
  <li>Engine, bearing, and axle anomaly classification</li>
  <li>Human-speech and unrelated-audio filtering</li>
  <li>FastAPI-based inference workflow</li>
  <li>Flutter mobile application integration</li>
</ul>

<strong>Technologies</strong>

<h3>☕ Microservices-Based Café Management System</h3>

<p>
A full-stack café menu, order, stock, payment, and identity management platform developed as an academic team project using a scalable microservice architecture.
</p>

<p>
The system separates business capabilities into independently developed services. Requests are routed through a central API Gateway, while asynchronous communication, caching, authentication, and multiple databases support performance and maintainability.
</p>

<strong>System features</strong>

<ul>
  <li>Menu, category, order, stock, and payment management</li>
  <li>Admin and public user authorization</li>
  <li>API Gateway-based request routing</li>
  <li>CQRS and Clean Architecture</li>
  <li>Event-driven service communication</li>
  <li>Distributed data and caching infrastructure</li>
</ul>

<strong>Technologies</strong>

<p>
  <code>.NET 8</code>
  <code>ASP.NET Core</code>
